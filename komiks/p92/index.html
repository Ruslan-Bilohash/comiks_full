A Walk in the Forest — Tur i skogen

Dad, Mia and Leo go blueberry picking in the forest — and meet a real moose!

🇳🇴 Pappa, Mia og Leo går på tur i skogen og møter en ekte elg!

📖 Read the comic with Norwegian audio

Dialogue in Norwegian with English translation

Dad: I dag går vi på tur i skogen!Today we're going for a walk in the forest!

Mia: Hurra! Jeg tar med kurven.Hooray! I'll bring the basket.

Leo: Se, der er en sopp!Look, there's a mushroom!

Dad: Den soppen er giftig. Ikke spis den!That mushroom is poisonous. Don't eat it!

Mia: Her er det mange blåbær!There are lots of blueberries here!

Leo: Nam! De er så søte!Yum! They're so sweet!

Sound: KNAKK!CRACK!

Leo: Hva var det?What was that?

Mia: En elg! Den er kjempestor!A moose! It's huge!

Narrator: Så begynner det å regne.Then it starts to rain.

Dad: Vær stille. Nå går vi rolig hjem.Be quiet. Now we walk home calmly.

Dad: Nå skal vi drikke varm kakao.Now we're going to drink hot cocoa.

Mia: Det var en fin tur!That was a lovely walk!

Leo: Og vi så en ekte elg!And we saw a real moose!
