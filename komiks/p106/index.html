The 17th of May — 17. mai

Ruslan celebrates Norway's national day for the first time: the children's parade, bunads, flags and lots of ice cream.

🇳🇴 Ruslan feirer nasjonaldagen for første gang: barnetog, bunad, flagg og masse is.

📖 Read the comic with Norwegian audio

Dialogue in Norwegian with English translation

Narrator: 17. mai er Norges nasjonaldag.The 17th of May is Norway's national day.

Mia: Hipp hipp hurra for Norge!Hip hip hooray for Norway!

Ruslan: Hvorfor feirer dere i dag?Why are you celebrating today?

Dad: Norge fikk sin egen grunnlov 17. mai 1814.Norway got its own constitution on 17 May 1814.

Nora: Se, barnetoget kommer!Look, the children's parade is coming!

Leo: Alle barna går med flagg og synger.All the children walk with flags and sing.

Dad: Mange har på seg bunad i dag.Many people are wearing a bunad today.

Ruslan: Hva er en bunad?What is a bunad?

Dad: Det er en tradisjonell drakt fra et bestemt sted i Norge.It is a traditional costume from a particular place in Norway.

Ruslan: Den er veldig vakker!It is very beautiful!

Leo: På 17. mai får barna spise så mye is de vil!On the 17th of May children can eat as much ice cream as they want!

Mia: Jeg vil ha tre is og en pølse!I want three ice creams and a hot dog!
