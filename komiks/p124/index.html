The appraisal talk — Medarbeidersamtalen

Once a year every employee in Norway has a talk with their manager: what went well, what is hard and where to go next. Denys prepares, talks about his workload and courses — and carefully raises the question of salary.

🇳🇴 En gang i året har alle ansatte i Norge en medarbeidersamtale: hva som har gått bra, hva som er krevende og veien videre. Denys forbereder seg, snakker om arbeidsmengde og kurs – og tar forsiktig opp lønn.

Dialogue in Norwegian with English translation

Narrator: En gang i året har alle ansatte en medarbeidersamtale med lederen sin.Once a year every employee has an appraisal talk with their manager.

Teacher Ingrid: Velkommen, Denys. Sett deg. Hvordan har året vært for deg?Welcome, Denys. Have a seat. How has the year been for you?

Denys: Stort sett bra. Jeg har lært mye, særlig etter at jeg tok over prosjektet.Mostly good. I have learned a lot, especially after I took over the project.

Teacher Ingrid: Det har vi lagt merke til. Kundene er veldig fornøyde.We have noticed that. The clients are very pleased.

Teacher Ingrid: Er det noe som er krevende i hverdagen din?Is there anything demanding in your everyday work?

Denys: Jeg opplever at arbeidsmengden har økt ganske mye i høst.I feel that the workload has increased quite a lot this autumn.

Denys: Jeg rekker oppgavene, men jeg må ofte jobbe overtid på fredager.I manage the tasks, but I often have to work overtime on Fridays.

Teacher Ingrid: Takk for at du sier ifra. Da bør vi fordele oppgavene annerledes.Thank you for speaking up. Then we should distribute the tasks differently.

Teacher Ingrid: Hva ønsker du å utvikle deg videre i?What would you like to develop further?

Denys: Jeg vil gjerne ta et kurs i prosjektledelse, og på sikt lede et team.I would like to take a course in project management, and lead a team in time.

Teacher Ingrid: Det støtter jeg. Vi har budsjett til kurs til våren.I support that. We have a budget for courses in the spring.

Denys: Kan vi også snakke om lønn? Ansvaret mitt har blitt større.Can we also talk about salary? My responsibility has grown.

Teacher Ingrid: Helt greit å ta det opp. Lønn avgjøres i den årlige forhandlingen i mai.It is perfectly fine to raise that. Salary is decided in the annual negotiation in May.

Teacher Ingrid: Jeg skriver ned tre mål for neste år. Er du enig i dem?I will write down three goals for next year. Do you agree with them?

Denys: Ja, men jeg vil gjerne ha en tilbakemelding hvert kvartal.Yes, but I would like feedback every quarter.

Teacher Ingrid: Avtale. Det blir en god rutine for oss begge.Agreed. That will be a good routine for both of us.

Teacher Ingrid: Takk for en åpen og nyttig samtale, Denys.Thank you for an open and useful conversation, Denys.

Denys: Takk selv. Nå vet jeg hva jeg skal jobbe mot.Thank you too. Now I know what to work towards.
