Ticket and app — Billett og app

Musa takes the bus in a new city for the first time. He buys a ticket in the app, works out the zones and the child fare — and then an inspector gets on.

🇳🇴 Musa tar bussen i en ny by for første gang. Han kjøper billett i appen, finner ut av soner og barnebillett — og så kommer kontrolløren.

📖 Read the comic with Norwegian audio

Dialogue in Norwegian with English translation

Musa: Jeg må kjøpe billett i appen før vi går på.I have to buy a ticket in the app before we get on.

Younger daughter: Trenger jeg egen billett, pappa?Do I need my own ticket, Dad?

Musa: Ja, men barn under seks år reiser gratis.Yes, but children under six travel for free.

Younger daughter: Jeg er sju. Da må du kjøpe barnebillett.I am seven. Then you have to buy a child ticket.

Musa: Appen spør om sone. Hvilken sone er vi i?The app asks about the zone. Which zone are we in?

Narrator: Han ser på kartet i appen: sone 1.He looks at the map in the app: zone 1.

Kari the driver: God dag, billettkontroll. Kan jeg få se billetten?Good day, ticket inspection. May I see your ticket?

Musa: Ja, her. Den er aktivert for to minutter siden.Yes, here. It was activated two minutes ago.

Kari the driver: Alt i orden. Husk å aktivere før du går om bord.All in order. Remember to activate before you board.

Musa: Hva skjer hvis man glemmer det?What happens if you forget?

Kari the driver: Da blir det gebyr — elleve hundre kroner.Then there is a fine — eleven hundred kroner.

Younger daughter: Oi! Pappa, kjøp alltid billett!Wow! Dad, always buy a ticket!
