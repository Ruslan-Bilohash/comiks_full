At the dentist — Hos tannlegen

Denys has toothache. The dentist finds a cavity, explains the treatment and the price, and reminds him that dental care is free for children under eighteen.

🇳🇴 Denys har vondt i en tann. Tannlegen finner et hull, forklarer behandlingen og prisen, og minner om at barn under atten år får gratis tannbehandling.

📖 Read the comic with Norwegian audio

Dialogue in Norwegian with English translation

Kari the driver: God dag, Denys. Hva er problemet i dag?Good afternoon, Denys. What is the problem today?

Denys: Jeg har hatt vondt i en tann i over en uke.I have had a toothache for over a week.

Kari the driver: Gjør det vondt når du drikker noe kaldt?Does it hurt when you drink something cold?

Denys: Ja, og når jeg tygger på venstre side.Yes, and when I chew on the left side.

Kari the driver: Jeg tar et røntgenbilde … Her er det et lite hull.I will take an X-ray … There is a small cavity here.

Denys: Må tannen trekkes?Does the tooth have to be pulled out?

Kari the driver: Nei da. Vi borer og legger en fylling i dag.Not at all. We will drill and put in a filling today.

Denys: Gjør det vondt?Does it hurt?

Kari the driver: Du får bedøvelse først, så kjenner du bare litt trykk.You get an anaesthetic first, so you will only feel a little pressure.

Denys: Hva koster behandlingen?How much does the treatment cost?

Kari the driver: Omtrent tusen kroner. Voksne betaler selv hos tannlegen.About a thousand kroner. Adults pay for the dentist themselves.

Kari the driver: Men barn under atten år får gratis behandling.But children under eighteen get free treatment.

Kari the driver: Ferdig! Husk å pusse tennene to ganger om dagen og bruke tanntråd.All done! Remember to brush twice a day and use dental floss.

Denys: Takk. Da bestiller jeg kontroll om et halvt år.Thank you. Then I will book a check-up in six months.
