774	½ Fractions
775	Fractions
776	parts of a whole, Norwegian names and a test
777	A fraction is a part of a whole: a pizza cut into 4 slices, you ate 3 — that is 3/4, in Norwegian “tre fjerdedeler”. Tap anything to hear it in Norwegian.
778	🍕 What is a fraction
779	The bottom number (nevner — denominator) says into how many equal parts the whole is cut. The top number (teller — numerator) says how many parts you take. Between them is the brøkstrek, the fraction bar.
780	teller — numerator: parts taken
781	nevner — denominator: equal parts in the whole
782	🧩 Fraction builder
783	Change the numbers to see the fraction on a pizza and a bar and hear its name.
784	🗣️ Fraction names
785	Most names are the ordinal number + “-del”: tredjedel, fjerdedel… Exceptions: en halv (1/2) and en tidel (1/10). For more than one part use “-deler”: to tredjedeler.
786	⚖️ Equal fractions
787	Different numbers, the same amount: 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6 = 4/8 = 5/10. Compare the coloured bars.
788	🍎 A fraction of a number
789	To find 1/3 of 12, split 12 into 3 equal groups — each group has 4. And 2/3 is two such groups, so 8.
