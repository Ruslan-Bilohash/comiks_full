1	<b>The offline app collects nothing at all</b> <b>Komiks·Lab</b> (<code>com.bilohash.komikslab</code>) contains every story, test and recording inside the installed app. It has no accounts and no network code — the modules that would talk to a server are not merely switched off, they are not included in the build. It works in airplane mode. Nothing leaves your device, so the rest of this policy applies only to the website and to the online app.
2	Who we are
3	Komiks·Lab is a Norwegian-learning project: a website at <a href="https://bilohash.com/comiks/">bilohash.com/comiks</a> and two Android apps. It is run by Ruslan Bilohash, an individual developer.
4	Contact for any privacy question, including data deletion: <a href="mailto:email@bilohash.com">email@bilohash.com</a>. We answer within 30 days.
5	What the website and the online app collect
6	This covers <a href="https://bilohash.com/comiks/">bilohash.com/comiks</a> and <b>Komiks·Lab Online</b> (<code>com.bilohash.komikslab.online</code>).
7	Only if you create an account
8	Data
9	Why

18	Your password is never stored. We keep only a one-way hash of it (Argon2id), which cannot be turned back into the password.
19	Without an account
20	Your settings and progress stay in your own browser (<code>localStorage</code>) and never reach us.
21	A session cookie <code>kl_s</code> is set only after you sign in. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and no third-party trackers.
22	The server keeps a short-lived, hashed form of your IP address to limit abuse (rate limiting). It is not linked to a person and is not used for anything else.
23	Anonymous visit statistics (our own, no third parties): which sections and comics are opened, the device type, interface language, the website you came from and the hour. Daily unique visitors are counted with a one-day salted hash of the IP address and browser; the IP itself is never stored and the salt is replaced and erased every day, so a visitor cannot be recognised on the next day. No cookies. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.
24	Technical crash reports: if a page breaks, the browser sends us the error text, the file and line, the section of the site, the build version and the browser and system type — no IP address, cookies or link to a person. Kept for up to 30 days to fix bugs.

46	How long we keep it
47	<b>Account and progress</b> — until you delete the account.
48	<b>Messages and wall posts</b> — until you or a moderator delete them.
49	<b>Sign-in sessions</b> — 30 days, then they expire.
50	<b>Successful sign-in history</b> — IP address, time, sign-in method and device/browser for 30 days. The IP is encrypted at rest, visible only to administrators for account security, and erased with the account. This is separate from anonymous visit statistics.
51	<b>Rate-limiting records</b> — hours, then they are overwritten.
52	<b>Spoken practice</b> — the audio is not recorded or stored on our side.
53	Your rights
54	Under the GDPR you may ask for a copy of your data, ask to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
55	<b>Delete your account yourself:</b> open your profile in the app or on the site → <i>Account</i> → <i>Delete account</i>. This removes the account, progress, friends and messages.
56	<b>Or write to <a href="mailto:email@bilohash.com">email@bilohash.com</a></b> and we will do it for you.
57	You may also complain to your national data protection authority.
58	Children
59	The apps are rated for everyone, but accounts, friends and messages are meant for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly create accounts for younger children. A parent who believes their child made an account can write to us and we will remove it.
60	Younger learners can use the <b>offline app</b>, which has no account and sends nothing anywhere.
61	Security
62	All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. The database and the configuration with credentials sit outside the public web folder and are closed to direct access. Only the site owner has administrative access.
63	Changes
64	If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the update is announced on the “What's new” page of the site.
